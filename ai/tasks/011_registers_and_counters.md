# Task: Registers and Counters (Phase 2)

## Objective
Implement specialized shapes for multi-bit registers and counters.

## Requirements
- Implement `REGISTER` shape with multi-bit input/output buses.
- Implement `COUNTER` shape with control pins (reset, enable, load).
- Support internal labeling for these blocks (e.g., "8-bit Reg").

## Deliverables
- Updated `shapes` and `renderer` packages.
- Sample diagrams with complex sequential logic.
